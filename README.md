# Hospital-Emergency-Room-Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard** is an interactive data analytics project designed to monitor and analyze emergency room operations. It provides insights into patient admissions, waiting times, patient satisfaction, referral patterns, and department performance.

The dashboard helps healthcare professionals identify operational bottlenecks, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and make data-driven decisions to improve patient flow and hospital efficiency.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze emergency room patient admissions and visit trends.
* Monitor average patient waiting times and admission efficiency.
* Evaluate patient satisfaction and service quality.
* Identify peak admission periods and patient demographics.
* Analyze referral patterns across different departments.
* Support data-driven decision-making for hospital operations.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following metrics:

* **Total Patients:** Number of patients visiting the emergency room.
* **Average Waiting Time:** Average time patients spend waiting for medical attention.
* **Patient Satisfaction Score:** Average patient satisfaction rating.
* **Admission Status:** Distribution of admitted and non-admitted patients.
* **Patient Referral:** Number of patients referred to different departments.
* **Patient Demographics:** Analysis based on age and gender.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Patient Overview

* Total number of patients visiting the emergency room.
* Patient distribution by gender and age group.
* Monthly and daily patient admission trends.

### 2. Waiting Time Analysis

* Average patient waiting time.
* Waiting time trends across different periods.
* Identification of potential delays in patient care.

### 3. Admission Analysis

* Comparison of admitted and non-admitted patients.
* Analysis of patient admission patterns.
* Insights into emergency room workload.

### 4. Department Referral Analysis

* Distribution of patients referred to different departments.
* Identification of departments receiving the highest number of referrals.
* Analysis of patient flow across hospital departments.

### 5. Patient Satisfaction Analysis

* Monitoring patient satisfaction scores.
* Analysis of satisfaction trends.
* Identification of areas for potential service improvement.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, analysis, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and dashboard development.
* **Excel Slicers:** Interactive filtering and data exploration.
* **Data Visualization:** Charts, graphs, and KPI cards.

## 🔍 Data Analysis Process

1. **Data Collection:** Imported hospital emergency room patient data.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Checked for missing values, inconsistencies, and duplicate records.
3. **Data Transformation:** Organized patient data and created relevant categories for analysis.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Identified trends and patterns in patient visits, waiting times, and admissions.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Created interactive visualizations and KPI cards.
6. **Insights Generation:** Analyzed the results to identify operational trends and potential improvement areas.
